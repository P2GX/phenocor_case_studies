# LMNA

- MNA topped the global correlation scan by a wide margin: 259 patients and 205 FDR-significant HPO pairs, more than any other cohort in phenopacket-store (FBN1, our reference, had 52). It is also the canonical pleiotropic locus, a single gene producing diseases across largely disjoint organ-system axes: cardiac and skeletal muscle (Emery-Dreifuss MD 2, [OMIM:181350](https://omim.org/entry/181350); dilated cardiomyopathy 1A, [OMIM:115200](https://omim.org/entry/115200)), metabolic (Dunnigan familial partial lipodystrophy, [OMIM:151660](https://omim.org/entry/151660)), and progeroid forms. Large n plus near-disjoint subtypes is exactly the regime where synergy analysis is informative, so this is the one cohort we develop as a full case study rather than a single figure.

**Correlation structure.** The raw co-occurrence matrix is dominated by one dense, near-perfectly correlated block: proximal and distal muscle weakness, joint / Achilles / elbow contracture, and skeletal muscle atrophy, several pairs at r = 1.0. This is the classic Emery-Dreifuss constellation and represents the redundant within-axis cluster: once you know one of these features, the others add little.

**Synergy.** Run against disease-subtype targets, synergy consistently pairs a feature from one axis with a feature from the other, which is where the subtype information actually lives.

- Muscular / cardiac target (EDMD2 / DCM): the strongest synergy sits between the myopathic axis and the lipodystrophy axis. Proximal muscle weakness x Lipodystrophy (S = 0.36), Distal muscle weakness x Lipodystrophy (S = 0.36), Muscle weakness x Lipodystrophy (S = 0.31), Elevated creatine kinase x Lipodystrophy (S = 0.26). Each term alone barely predicts the subtype; jointly they are highly informative because they sit on opposite ends of the LMNA spectrum.
- Lipodystrophy target (FPLD2): a smaller, cardiac-conduction and contracture signature. Achilles tendon contracture x Atrial fibrillation (S = 0.12), Dilated cardiomyopathy x First-degree AV block (S = 0.06), Atrial fibrillation x Joint contracture (S = 0.06). The Emery-Dreifuss-type conduction-plus-contracture combination jointly separates FPLD from the muscular and cardiac forms.

**Conclusion:** LMNA phenotypes resolve onto a myopathic-cardiac axis versus a lipodystrophic-metabolic axis. Correlation captures the dense within-axis redundancy (the EDMD cluster), while synergy captures the cross-axis combinations that carry subtype identity: muscle weakness plus lipodystrophy status jointly resolves the spectrum far better than either does alone. Next steps worth pursuing here are variant-effect stratification (truncating vs missense) and pulling in the progeroid subset as a third target.
